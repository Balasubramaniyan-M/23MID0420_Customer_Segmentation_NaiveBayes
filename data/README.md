## 4. Data Quality Audit Findings
- **Exact Duplicate Rows**: 0 rows
- **Duplicate Feature Profiles**: 734 rows (customers sharing identical non-ID predictor features but evaluated independently without data leakage)
- **Label Provenance & Circularity Audit**: Verified that no feature directly encodes the historical segmentation assignment rules or was measured post-assignment. All predictors represent pre-assignment demographic, psychographic, and behavioral attributes.
- **Psychographic Measurement Provenance**:
  - `Spending_Score`: Survey-derived or historical tier score. Subject to self-reporting bias.
  - `Var_1`: Anonymized lifestyle/preference category assigned externally. Treated with extra caution due to unknown internal semantics.

## 5. Excluded Direct Identifiers & Privacy Controls
- Direct identifier `ID` is excluded from all predictor feature sets (`demographic`, `psychographic`, `behavioral`).
- `ID` is retained strictly for output file traceability (`test_predictions.csv` and `error_analysis.csv`).
- **Privacy & Intended-Use Boundary**: Predictions generated by this system are supervised classifications of historical marketing segment labels. They do NOT constitute verified customer preferences, psychological ground truth, or individual intent. Models must NEVER be used as the sole basis for autonomous sensitive decisions, price discrimination, credit/service denial, or unfair exclusion. Human oversight is mandatory.
